# Product brief

## Problem

A reader encounters unfamiliar notation or concepts, leaves the book to ask an AI, loses context, and may get an explanation that assumes the very foundations they lack. Discussing the same passage with another learner is often disconnected from the book.

## Intended change

Help the learner bridge a specific obstacle and return to meaningful reading with more independence. A fluent explanation, chat volume or streak does not establish learning.

## First proposed learner and journey

Adult technical learner studying language models and encountering gaps in maths. Example: attention → dot products → matrix dimensions, with a short checked example and optional changed-example question. This is illustrative, not a tested learner outcome or complete curriculum.

## Core experience

- Library and accessible reader with import, position, bookmarks and private annotations.
- Highlight → preview quote/context → ask for explanation, prerequisites or maths walkthrough.
- Bounded learning bridge, source reference, notation/dimensions and return-to-passage action.
- Distinguish self-reported difficulty, assisted answers and delayed unaided performance.
- Save/resume private reading offline; live offline AI not promised.
- Explicit sharing with one willing partner first; eventual same-edition invite-only discussion, not a public feed.

## Trust boundaries

Private by default. No default whole-book upload. Only authorized DRM-free material; no DRM circumvention or book redistribution. AI content labelled; no silent source, provider or billing substitution. Learner controls depth, pausing and reminders.

## Success to investigate

Reliable passage restoration, accurate equation input, useful support, ability to return, delayed independent explanation, and voluntary continuation. Small pilots provide usability/directional evidence, not causal efficacy claims. A trusted reference/reviewer is needed for maths feedback.
